1 Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Rox Player ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the Rox Player mobile application on Android devices.

Rox Player is an offline multimedia utility providing HD video playback, local music playback, local Video-to-MP3 audio extraction, WhatsApp/WA Business local Status saving, and an encrypted on-device Secret Vault. We built the App around strict data minimization: all core operations execute locally on your hardware without remote servers.

2 Information We Do Not Collect

Rox Player does not require registration, sign-in, or an internet connection to function. We do not collect, harvest, sell, rent, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal Identifiers: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or government IDs.
  • Media Content & Metadata: Your videos, songs, thumbnails, folder names, or hidden Vault files are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Usage Analytics & Telemetry: We do not integrate third-party analytics trackers, behavioral profiling SDKs, or ad-targeting networks.
  • Device Tracking IDs: No Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), IMEI, MAC address, or precise GPS location data is collected.
App Store Data Safety Summary: Rox Player collects zero user data and shares zero user data with third parties. All preferences (such as theme selection, favorites, and Vault configuration) are stored exclusively in local device storage.

3 Device Permissions & Why They Are Requested

To function as a local file-based media player and organizer on modern Android versions (including Android 13, 14, and above), Rox Player requests only the permissions strictly necessary to read and save user-selected media on your device:

Permission Purpose in Rox Player
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES		 Used on Android 13+ to discover and list local videos, music tracks, album thumbnails, and status images stored on your phone for offline playback.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Used on Android 12 and below to scan local folders and save extracted MP3 files or saved Status items to your public Music and Pictures directories.
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Requested optionally to enable full file-management capabilities: moving files into and out of the encrypted Secret Vault (hiding originals from public gallery) and reading locally cached status media folders.
WAKE_LOCK
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Keeps your screen awake while watching videos and ensures smooth background audio playback when switching apps or locking the screen.

You may revoke any permission at any time in your Android System Settings. Revoking storage permissions will only prevent the App from scanning or saving local files until granted again.

4 How Core Features Process Local Data

A. Video & Music Playback

Rox Player scans standard device directories (such as Movies, DCIM, Download, and Music) solely to display playable files inside the App. App preferences (like Light/Dark theme, favorite tracks, and playback settings) are persisted locally via Android SharedPreferences and never leave your phone.

B. Secret Vault & PIN Protection

When you place a video or audio file into the Secret Vault, the file is encrypted locally on your device using AES-256 encryption derived from your 4-digit PIN and stored inside the App's protected storage area.

  • A unique offline Recovery Key is generated locally on your device when you set up your PIN so you can reset a forgotten PIN without deleting your encrypted files and without any server or internet dependency.
  • Because zero data is sent to our servers, neither we nor any third party can decrypt your Vault or recover a lost PIN + Recovery Key combination.

C. Video to MP3 Extractor

Audio extraction runs 100% locally on your device. Extracted MP3 tracks are saved directly to your local storage under /Music/RoxPlayer/ so they appear immediately in your Music library.

D. Status Saver (Local Media Cache)

The Status Saver feature reads locally cached status photo and video folders already stored on your device by installed messaging apps (such as WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business) only after you grant storage permission. Saved items are copied locally to /Pictures/RoxPlayer_Status/.

Disclaimer: Rox Player is an independent offline utility and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc. Users are responsible for respecting the copyright and privacy of media owners before saving or sharing statuses.

5 Network Usage & Third-Party Interactions

Rox Player operates entirely offline by default. The App does not run background network syncs, telemetry pings, or remote configuration fetches.

  • System Share Sheet: When you tap the Share button on a song, video, or saved status, Android's native share chooser is invoked so you can send the file to an app of your choice. Rox Player does not observe or log what you share.
  • External Intents: If you open a video file from your file manager using Rox Player, playback happens strictly locally on your device.

6 Data Security, Retention & Deletion

Because Rox Player maintains no external servers or user databases, we retain none of your personal information. All app settings, thumbnail caches, and encrypted Vault data reside solely on your device:

  • You can clear temporary thumbnails at any time in Settings → Clear Thumbnail Cache.
  • Before uninstalling Rox Player, remember to Unhide / Restore any files inside the Secret Vault if you wish to keep them in your public gallery, as Android deletes app-private encrypted storage upon uninstallation.

7 Children's Privacy

Rox Player is safe for general audiences and complies with children's privacy regulations (including COPPA and GDPR-K) because the App does not collect, request, or store personal data from any user of any age.
